# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith.
- Disagree about the work: "this breaks when the tree is truncated" rather than
  "you clearly did not think about this".
- Accept that people work at different levels and at different speeds. This
  project is maintained by someone learning as they go, and beginners' questions
  are welcome here.
- Say why. A review comment without a reason is an instruction; with one it is
  an explanation.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone rather than their argument.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Comments belittling someone for their experience, background, identity or
  first language.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is taking
part in this project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile, or open an
issue if the matter can be discussed publicly. Reports are handled privately.

Consequences are proportionate: usually a request to change tone, and in
serious or repeated cases a block from the repository.

## Note

This is a short document written for a small project rather than an adopted
standard text. If it turns out not to cover something, that is a gap in the
document, not permission.
